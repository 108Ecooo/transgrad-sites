Why we call ourselves an integrator, not a carrier

On how TRANSGRAD works, what we believe in and why clients stay with us for years.

Alexey Gromov · General Director, TRANSGRAD LLC

When people ask me what TRANSGRAD does, I never answer «transport». Transport is what you see from the outside: a truck, a cargo, a waybill. What we actually do begins much earlier and ends much later.

We embed into the client's processes. We study their production cycle, procurement schedule, seasonality and deadline requirements. Only then do we start planning logistics — not «a trip from A to B», but a supply system that runs for months and years without failures.

Not a contractor — part of the team

The difference between a carrier and an integrator is simple. You send a carrier a request and hope everything goes well. An integrator knows what you need to ship and when, because it is immersed in your processes as deeply as your own logistics department.

That is why companies for whom supply stability is critical have worked with us for years: GAZ Group, PARTKOM, NHL Group. Their logistics is our zone of responsibility. All of it.

If we take on a cargo, we deliver it as if it were our own. Behind every route are people who don't sleep while the truck is on the road.

Difficult is our specialisation

We deliberately chose to work on routes where there are no ready-made solutions: the South Caucasus, Iran, Central Asia, Mongolia, China. Here the rules change on the fly, the infrastructure is unpredictable and the cost of a mistake is high. This is exactly where you find out who is worth what in logistics.

When others turn a cargo down because of a «difficult destination», we sit down and calculate the route. A solution is almost always found — it is a matter of experience, connections on the ground and the willingness to take responsibility.

What we believe in

  • Trust is earned with trips, not presentations. Every truck delivered on time is a brick in the foundation of the relationship.
  • A problem must be solved before the client learns about it. If you are the one calling us first, it means we have fallen short somewhere.
  • Honesty is cheaper than workarounds. We do not promise the impossible and we always tell it like it is — even when the truth is inconvenient.

If your company is tired of «request-based» logistics and wants a partner who will take international supply off your hands — let's talk. I am confident we will find a common language.

Alexey Gromov
General Director, TRANSGRAD LLC
